<threat_model>
- **T-01-01 (HIGH, scientific integrity):** Many-to-many merges or array cross-products inflate paired wins. Mitigation: validate uniqueness and join exact natural cells per D-02.
- **T-01-02 (HIGH, publication integrity):** A failing method disappears from hard datasets and improves its headline rank. Mitigation: complete common support per D-01 plus separate reliability outputs.
- **T-01-03 (HIGH, statistical validity):** Pooled fold ranks or unequal resample counts weight datasets differently. Mitigation: aggregate within dataset before ranking/rating per D-03.
- **T-01-04 (MEDIUM, transparency):** A single failure rate hides invalid, fallback, timeout, and missing evidence. Mitigation: machine-queryable outcome counts and reasons.
</threat_model>
